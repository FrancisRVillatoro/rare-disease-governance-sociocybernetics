# v1.1.0 — additive computational reproducibility checks

This cumulative release retains all source code, parameters and canonical
results of v1.0.0 unchanged. It **does not** include any manuscript, article
PDF/DOCX or correspondence. The MIT licence and all six creators are retained.

New material:

1. Frozen post-convergence unregularised best-response audit, 432 nominal
   actor-period cases, two searches per case (864 response problems), with
   source identity, full forecasts, multipliers, optimiser traces, deviations,
   stationarity/complementarity diagnostics and machine-readable raw results.
2. Executable provenance for the five first-period convergence-screen settings
   (Supplementary Table S6).
3. Executable historical dense/sparse sequential variant comparisons and the
   full sparse-map cyclic/reversed-order audit (Supplementary S11/Table S15).
   A non-exhaustive dense-map order spot check additionally shows a regulatory
   allocation of 0.4448 in one cyclic shift; therefore an older statement
   that the dense-map allocation lies in 0.05--0.30 across update orders
   must not be interpreted as a universal range.

The audit found small remaining unilateral improvements at finite stopping
tolerances. It **does not prove variational equilibrium**, global optimality
or uniqueness. No original simulations have been re-tuned or changed.
